FareCheckr Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

FareCheckr ("the app") helps riders compare GPS-tracked auto-rickshaw routes against official regional fare rates, so they can identify overcharging and, if they choose, file a complaint with the relevant transport authority. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

In short
  • There are no accounts, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or email.
  • The places you search for and the locations you travel to are not stored by us. They pass through our server only to get an answer from Google.
  • We keep a small amount of anonymous statistics (counts and fare numbers) that cannot be linked to you.
  • Your ride history stays on your phone.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show advertisements.

Location data

FareCheckr uses your device's location to:

  • Suggest your current position as the pickup when you plan a ride.
  • Track your actual GPS route during an active ride, so it can be compared against the recommended route and used to calculate a fair fare.

Route tracking only works while FareCheckr is open and in the foreground. If you switch apps or lock your phone during a ride, tracking pauses until you return to the app. Tracking stops automatically when the ride ends or is cancelled. You can refuse the location permission and still use the app by entering the pickup manually; the actual route then cannot be tracked.

What is sent to our server, and what happens to it

To show search results, routes and addresses, the app sends requests to the FareCheckr server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards them to Google and returns Google's answer. Depending on what you do, a request contains:

  • the text you type when searching for a place, and your approximate position to rank nearby results;
  • the pickup and drop coordinates of a trip you are planning;
  • a coordinate, when the app looks up the address of a point on the map;
  • at the end of a ride, your GPS route (a list of coordinates), so it can be matched to roads for an accurate distance.

The FareCheckr server does not store this information. It is used only to answer the request. Google processes it under its own terms (see "Third-party services"). Our own logs record only whether a request succeeded or failed, never the text, places or coordinates. Cloudflare, as our hosting provider, may keep short-lived technical logs of connections (such as time, result and network address) under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy; we do not store IP addresses ourselves.

If the server cannot be reached, or a daily limit has been reached, the app falls back to free OpenStreetMap services (search and routing). In that case the text you search for and the coordinates involved are sent directly from your phone to those public services, which handle them under their own policies (see below).

Anonymous statistics

To understand how the app is used and to improve it, the app sends anonymous statistics:

  • Events: that the app was opened, a ride was started, or a complaint was started, with the city and app version.
  • A summary of each finished ride, using numbers only: the distance (rounded to the nearest half kilometre), the recommended fare, the fare on the route you actually travelled, the amount you were charged, whether it was night time, a return trip or included a one-way surcharge, whether you went on to a complaint, the city, the app version and the date.

These summaries contain no locations, addresses, place names, vehicle numbers, contact details or device identifiers, and they cannot be linked to you or to your phone. Ride summaries are kept for up to about 13 months; event counts are kept only as totals.

Installation code

The first time the app runs, our server gives it a random code. The app attaches this code to its requests so the server can limit how many requests one installation can make each day, which protects the service from misuse and keeps it free. The code is not linked to your name, phone or accounts. The daily counters that use it are deleted after 7 days.

Ride and complaint data

Ride details (pickup and drop locations, distances, fares, and the autorickshaw number if you enter it) are stored only on your device so you can review your ride history within the app. The app keeps your most recent rides and you can clear them at any time.

If you choose to file a complaint, the app pre-fills an email with your ride details and opens your device's email app so you can review and send it yourself to the relevant transport authority. FareCheckr does not send this email on your behalf and does not see its contents.

Third-party services

The app relies on the following services to function:

  • Google Maps Platform (Maps, Places, Routes, Roads and Geocoding) — to display maps, search for places, calculate routes, snap tracked routes to roads and find addresses. The map itself is loaded directly from Google by your phone. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the FareCheckr server. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and OSRM) — used only as a free fallback when the FareCheckr server is unavailable. Governed by the OpenStreetMap Foundation's Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub — hosts this policy and, for older versions of the app, a small file used to tell an outdated app to update.

FareCheckr does not currently show advertisements. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first to disclose it.

Security

All communication between the app and the FareCheckr server uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our secret keys are kept on the server and are not included in the app.

Data retention and deletion

  • On your phone: ride history stays until you delete it in the app, uninstall the app, or clear its storage.
  • Search text, places, coordinates and GPS routes: not stored by FareCheckr.
  • Installation-code counters: deleted after 7 days.
  • Anonymous ride summaries: kept for up to about 13 months. Because they are not linked to you, we cannot identify or delete a particular person's summary.

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone, including the installation code. If you have any concern about your data, contact us using the address below.

Children's privacy

FareCheckr is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
