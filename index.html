FareCheckr Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

FareCheckr ("the app") helps riders in Kerala compare GPS-tracked auto-rickshaw routes against official RTO fare rates, so they can identify overcharging and, if they choose, file a complaint with the Kerala Motor Vehicles Department (MVD). This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

Location data

FareCheckr uses your device's location to:

  • Show your current position when you plan a ride.
  • Track your actual GPS route during an active ride, so it can be compared against the recommended route and used to calculate a fair fare.

With your permission, this tracking can continue in the background while a ride is active — for example if you lock your phone or switch apps — so the recorded route stays accurate for the whole trip. Background tracking only runs while a ride is in progress; it stops automatically when the ride ends or is cancelled.

Location data collected during a ride is stored only on your device. It is not sent to any FareCheckr server, because FareCheckr does not operate a backend server — the app works entirely on-device. Location data leaves your device only if you choose to file a complaint, in which case the relevant ride details (including pickup/drop addresses) are included in an email you send yourself, as described below.

Ride and complaint data

Ride details (pickup and drop locations, distances, fares, and the autorickshaw number if you enter it) are stored locally on your device so you can review your ride history within the app. This data is not transmitted to FareCheckr or any third party automatically.

If you choose to file a complaint, the app pre-fills an email with your ride details and opens your device's email app so you can review and send it yourself to the Kerala MVD's official complaints address (complaints.mvd@kerala.gov.in). FareCheckr does not send this email on your behalf or see its contents after you send it.

Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services to function:

  • Google Maps, Places, Routes, and Roads APIs — used to display maps, search for locations, calculate routes, and improve the accuracy of tracked distances. Your location and search queries are sent to Google as part of these requests, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

FareCheckr does not currently show advertisements. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first to disclose it.

Data retention and deletion

Ride history is stored on your device using standard app storage and remains until you delete it from within the app, uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage through your device settings. FareCheckr has no server-side copy to delete, because none is kept.

Children's privacy

FareCheckr is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
